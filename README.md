# DESI ERA

Create the complete premium e-commerce website for my women's ethnicwear brand HOK. This is the kurti website project we have already discussed. Do not build a generic fashion template; make it look like a polished real Indian fashion brand ready for launch.

BRAND + DIRECTION
- Brand shown primarily as “HOK” in the website header/navigation and visual identity.
- Use the approved second logo direction: a refined, minimal, feminine-modern HOK wordmark/monogram feel. HOK should be the dominant visible brand mark; use the full brand name only in selected places such as footer/about/brand story if needed.
- Color direction is already approved as a balanced mix: warm feminine neutrals with a sophisticated darker contrast and a subtle premium accent. Keep the palette elegant, soft, contemporary and wearable; avoid loud neon, overly pink styling, or excessive beige monotony.
- Hero model direction is approved: use a mix depending on the product, with real fashion-model imagery throughout the site rather than one repeated generic model image. Where product photography is unavailable, use tasteful editable image placeholders rather than fake-looking random stock images.

BUSINESS CATEGORY — STRICTLY
- Women’s Kurtis
- Women’s Kurtas
- Kurta Sets
- Co-ord Sets
Do NOT include sarees, lehengas, gowns, kidswear, menswear or unrelated categories.

WEBSITE GOAL
Build a conversion-focused fashion e-commerce storefront that feels premium but approachable, with strong product discovery, mobile-first usability, and a clear path from browsing to purchase. Include Indian e-commerce essentials: COD, shipping information, trust messaging, wishlist, cart, product search, filters and responsive checkout UI.

HOMEPAGE
Create a visually strong homepage with:
1. Slim announcement bar: “Free Shipping • Cash on Delivery Available”
2. Clean header with HOK logo, navigation: New Arrivals, Kurtis, Kurtas, Kurta Sets, Co-ord Sets, Sale; search, account, wishlist, cart icons.
3. Large editorial hero section using fashion-model imagery. Hero copy should be concise and brand-led, not generic business language. Suggested headline direction: “Everyday Indian, Effortlessly Elevated.” with supporting line about contemporary ethnicwear. CTAs: “Shop New Arrivals” and “Explore Sets”.
4. Category cards for the four approved categories.
5. New Arrivals product grid.
6. “Made for Your Everyday” editorial/model section showing versatile styling.
7. Best Sellers product grid.
8. “Why HOK” trust/value section: thoughtfully designed, easy-to-style silhouettes, quality-focused, everyday comfort; do not make unsupported claims about fabric or manufacturing.
9. Styling/editorial banner featuring model imagery.
10. Instagram/social-style visual gallery placeholder.
11. Newsletter/signup section.
12. Footer with HOK, Shop links, Help, Shipping & Returns, Contact, social icons and selected full brand-name usage.

SHOP / COLLECTION EXPERIENCE
- Dedicated collection/category pages for Kurtis, Kurtas, Kurta Sets and Co-ord Sets.
- Product grid with product image, name, price, compare-at/original price where applicable, sale badge, wishlist heart.
- Filters: size, color, price, category, availability; sorting: featured, newest, price low-high, price high-low.
- Search UI with useful empty/no-results state.
- Mobile filter drawer and responsive grid.

PRODUCT DETAIL PAGE
Include large image gallery, product name, price, sale information, ratings/reviews UI placeholder, size selector, size guide, quantity selector, Add to Cart, Buy Now, wishlist, delivery/pincode checker placeholder, COD availability message, shipping/returns information, product details, fabric/material/care placeholders, styling suggestion section, related products.
Do not fabricate specific fabric composition, ratings, reviews, delivery promises or product claims. Use editable placeholder content where actual catalog data is not supplied.

CART + CHECKOUT
- Slide-out or dedicated cart with product thumbnails, sizes, quantity controls, remove/save for later, subtotal, shipping note, COD message, coupon field and checkout CTA.
- Checkout UI with contact, shipping address, delivery method, payment method including Cash on Delivery and online payment placeholder. Make it a realistic e-commerce flow, but do not connect a payment provider unless explicitly requested.

ESSENTIAL PAGES
- Home
- Shop / All Products
- Kurtis
- Kurtas
- Kurta Sets
- Co-ord Sets
- New Arrivals
- Product Detail
- Cart
- Wishlist
- About HOK
- Contact
- Shipping & Delivery
- Returns & Exchange
- FAQ
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions

DESIGN SYSTEM
- Premium Indian contemporary fashion aesthetic.
- Strong editorial typography: elegant serif for selected display headlines paired with a clean modern sans-serif for navigation/body.
- Generous whitespace, refined product cards, subtle borders, sophisticated hover states, restrained motion and smooth transitions.
- Use a balanced neutral + dark contrast palette with a subtle premium accent. Keep accessibility and contrast strong.
- Product cards should feel clean and luxury-fashion inspired, not marketplace-like.
- Avoid huge rounded cards everywhere, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, neon colors, cartoon illustrations, fake reviews, fake testimonials, fake statistics, or clutter.
- Use tasteful model photography with varied poses/crops and product-focused imagery.

CONTENT / BRAND VOICE
- Feminine, confident, effortless, modern Indian.
- Short, polished copy. No “helping small businesses” or digital-marketing language.
- Do not invent founder history, awards, customer counts, sustainability claims, fabric claims, certifications or results.

FUNCTIONALITY
- Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts.
- Working navigation, category routes, product detail routes, cart interactions, wishlist interactions, search/filter/sort UI, quantity controls and checkout flow UI.
- Use a realistic editable sample catalog with products limited to the four approved categories. Clearly structure product data so I can replace it later.
- Add SEO-ready metadata and semantic accessible HTML.
- Make all key content easy to edit.
- Use reusable TypeScript/React components with Tailwind and shadcn/ui.
- Optimize performance and mobile experience.

IMPORTANT: This is the first production-quality build. Make design decisions yourself where details are unspecified, while preserving the approved brand direction above. Do not ask me to repeat the requirements. Build the website now and make it visually impressive from the first preview.
